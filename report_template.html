You might not need a GPU

GPUs are expensive, and on shared clusters there are far fewer of them than CPUs. They earn their cost when a model has to run a huge number of times: training it, processing a large dataset, serving heavy traffic, or generating output step by step the way language and diffusion models do.

But not every inference workload looks like that. For a mid-sized model handling one request at a time, a handful of CPU cores may be all you need.
